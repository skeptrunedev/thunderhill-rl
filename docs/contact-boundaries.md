# Consistent contact at pavement boundaries

A full circuit diagnostic samples both sides of every track station immediately inside and outside the pavement edge, around the curb limit, and farther off road. The original code failed 13 of its 27,648 samples with undefined ground height. Those failures were reproduced in an isolated checkout of `e9a8d0d` before the contact refactor.

The mesh did not have a hole at the first failing point, x 7.99179458618164 and z 69.0894088745117. Independent polygon classification on both original and float32 coordinates placed it inside the road cutout. Godot's runtime `Geometry2D.is_point_in_polygon` instead returned false for both rings. Its oblique ray could count both edges adjoining a distant shared vertex. Increasing terrain triangle tolerance would have extended terrain into the road and hidden the classification error.

Cutout membership now uses scalar float64 horizontal ray crossing with half open endpoint ownership and explicit exact boundary membership. Each shared vertex belongs to one crossing. No vertices, triangle tolerances or road widths were changed. Unit checks cover concavity, holes, horizontal edges, vertices, winding reversal, coordinate translation, repeated closing vertices and the actual road ring regression.

Height and normal also now come from one surface selection and one terrain triangle search. Previously, height classified the edge using a Vector2 length while normal selection used the separately calculated nearest segment distance. Their rounding could disagree at exact edges. Among the diagnostic records, 67 normals now match the surface chosen for height, and 13 formerly undefined heights are valid. All remaining records are byte identical to the baseline. The corrected full record digest is `cc513b934122185d4b75dc7d99880d3368c4e4f357238cfe36c583981f3520f4`.

Run `godot --headless --path godot --script tests/check_contact_samples.gd`. An optional `-- --contact-output=/absolute/path/records.jsonl` records each complete variant byte encoding plus readable values for comparison. The final diagnostic reports zero failures. The independent track contact audit also passes 475 far terrain samples, 2,222 visible triangle samples and 31 road centre samples. The rendered human control diagnostic passes with zero failures. These checks validate this contact correction, not the full motorcycle dynamics model.

The conservative diagnostic driver then completed a full legal lap through the real agent API: 40,383 recorded ticks, all 32 ordered gates, zero offroad ticks and no crash. The simulation covered 336.525 seconds. Its report is `artifacts/qa/contact-boundary-lap/summary.json`. This uses privileged track geometry for regression testing, not a learned policy, and does not claim optimal racing. The explicit environment failure and reset diagnostic also passes.
